Python analytics · Portfolio project

Credit Risk Prediction

Explores borrower patterns associated with loan delinquency in a dataset of 500 borrower records (80 delinquent). Compares Logistic Regression and Random Forest with SMOTE applied inside the training pipelines.

Key lesson: Random Forest matched the baseline's 84% accuracy but identified no delinquent cases in the test set, while Logistic Regression had lower accuracy (74%) and detected 25% of them. Accuracy alone can hide poor detection of the minority class.

Limitations: Small dataset and exploratory models; not production-ready. The Forage (Tata iQ) job simulation and my independent Python modelling are separate activities.
